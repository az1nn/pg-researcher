# Case 001 — The 48 Laws of Power as a Transformation Test

## Why this case exists

This is not the first religious source. It is the first **methodological stress test** for PG Religion Research because it forces the pipeline to distinguish:

- what Robert Greene's book argues;
- what a third-party video says about Greene;
- what the project accepts, rejects or reverses;
- what PG Religion Research turns into an original ethical/spiritual synthesis.

## Seed video

- Title: *As 48 Leis do Poder: o manual que escancara a sombra humana*
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NKF2v9jWeBw
- Current source status: `research_only`
- Captured public-description thesis: the video reframes selected laws away from power over other people and toward personal power, consciousness, character and leadership.
- Open metadata gates: channel/creator, publication date and transcript/structured notes.

No statement from the public description is treated as a statement by Robert Greene unless checked against the book or an authoritative edition/source.

## Underlying book

- Robert Greene, *The 48 Laws of Power*.
- First publication: 1998 (Viking/Profile Books editions are documented by publisher/catalog sources).
- Rights: protected modern work; research/brief quotation only unless separate authorization exists.
- The publisher describes the work as a manual concerned with gaining, observing and defending against control/power and presents the laws as oriented toward domination, prudence, boldness and self-preservation.

## Research question

**Can a text about external power be transformed into a disciplined study of sovereignty over the self without falsely attributing the transformation to the original author?**

## Proposed PG transformation model

For each selected law:

1. **Original law** — identify the actual proposition in Greene.
2. **Mechanism** — what social/psychological dynamic does the law notice?
3. **Shadow** — what vice, fear or distortion appears when the mechanism becomes domination?
4. **Ethical inversion** — what changes when the locus of power moves from controlling others to governing oneself?
5. **Traditional correspondence** — only after independent research, compare the resulting theme with a Hermetic, Pythagorean, scriptural or philosophical source.
6. **PG synthesis** — formulate an original teaching with explicit provenance.

## Example hypothesis — not yet a factual claim

A law about controlling appearances may produce a PG inquiry into **self-knowledge versus image-management**. That can later be compared to Hermetic ideas of ignorance/knowledge or Pythagorean self-examination, but similarity alone will not establish historical influence.

## Claim typing for this case

| Statement | Required type |
| --- | --- |
| “Greene's law says X” | `source_fact` |
| “The publisher characterizes the book as X” | `source_fact` |
| “The video reframes the law as X” | `source_fact` about the video |
| “This resembles Hermetic theme Y” | `comparative_parallel` |
| “Therefore Greene drew from Hermeticism” | historical/influence claim requiring independent evidence |
| “PG interprets power as sovereignty of the self” | `pg_editorial_synthesis` |

## Definition of done

This case is ready to become an e-book chapter only when:

- the exact selected laws are checked against an authorized/legitimate copy or authoritative publisher metadata;
- video metadata and structured notes are captured;
- Hermetic/Pythagorean comparison claims are sourced independently;
- every quote has a rights basis;
- every PG inversion is visibly labeled as original synthesis.
