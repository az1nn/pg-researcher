# Spec 006 — PG Religion Research Foundation

## Status

Proposed foundation for the Prince' Gutt religious, philosophical and esoteric research/publishing line.

## Purpose

Extend `pg-researcher` from artist/public-source research into a distinct, evidence-backed corpus for **PG RELIGION RESEARCH**.

The project studies religious texts, classical philosophy, occult/esoteric works, folklore and popular wisdom in search of recurring concepts that can be presented through a Prince' Gutt editorial lens, with special attention to Hermetic and Pythagorean traditions.

The output is a reusable knowledge base and a production pipeline for original, transformative e-books.

## Editorial thesis

PG Religion Research is universalist in method, not homogenizing in scholarship.

It may identify analogies, resonances and conceptual bridges across traditions, but it MUST distinguish:

1. what a source explicitly says;
2. how historians or scholars contextualize it;
3. how a tradition interprets it internally;
4. where PG Religion Research proposes a comparative parallel;
5. where Prince' Gutt adds an original editorial synthesis.

A similarity is not evidence of historical dependence or secret common origin unless independently supported.

## Research lenses

Primary lenses:

- Hermeticism;
- Pythagoreanism and number/harmony traditions;
- Platonism and Neoplatonism;
- Abrahamic scriptures and mystical traditions;
- Indian philosophical/religious traditions;
- Buddhist traditions;
- Daoist/classical Chinese traditions;
- Egyptian, Greco-Roman and Near Eastern religious material;
- Western esotericism and occult literature;
- folklore, proverbs and popular wisdom.

The list is extensible and does not imply equivalence among traditions.

## Claim taxonomy

Every material assertion MUST be typed as one of:

- `source_fact` — directly supported by the cited primary/source text;
- `historical_context` — supported by reputable historical/academic scholarship;
- `tradition_claim` — a claim made within a religious/esoteric tradition, presented as such;
- `scholarly_interpretation` — an attributed academic interpretation;
- `comparative_parallel` — a project-authored comparison between independently sourced concepts;
- `pg_editorial_synthesis` — Prince' Gutt / PG Religion Research interpretation or teaching frame.

The system MUST NOT silently promote a `comparative_parallel` or `pg_editorial_synthesis` into historical fact.

## Source hierarchy

### R1 — Primary/canonical source

Ancient or historical texts, manuscripts, critical editions, official religious publications, public-domain editions/translations where rights are verified.

### R2 — Academic/critical scholarship

University presses, peer-reviewed research, recognized historians of religion, philologists and subject specialists.

### R3 — Tradition-internal commentary

Recognized theological, mystical, initiatory or doctrinal commentary. Valuable for understanding what adherents teach; not automatically neutral historical evidence.

### R4 — Modern esoteric/occult author

Modern occultists, perennialists, initiatory authors and related works. Claims MUST remain attributed to their authors/schools.

### R5 — Popular wisdom / oral / community material

Proverbs, customs, interviews, folklore and community traditions. Capture provenance, region and date where possible; do not fabricate antiquity.

## Copyright and publication gate

Every source record used for an e-book MUST have a rights status:

- `public_domain_text`;
- `licensed_or_authorized`;
- `quotation_only`;
- `research_only`;
- `unknown`.

Rules:

- `unknown` cannot enter a publication build.
- Copyrighted modern books may be researched and paraphrased, but must not be reproduced substantially.
- Ancient works are not enough to establish public-domain status for a modern translation, introduction, annotation or edition.
- Quotations should be brief unless a verified publication basis permits more.
- Original PG synthesis must be written from the evidence graph, not by mechanically rewriting a single modern source.

## E-book contract

Every e-book MUST include:

1. title and series identity;
2. editorial thesis;
3. source/method note;
4. historical context sufficient to prevent misleading attribution;
5. chapters built from traceable claims;
6. explicit distinction between source, interpretation and PG synthesis;
7. Hermetic/Pythagorean bridge when genuinely relevant;
8. reflective or contemplative application where appropriate;
9. notes/citations/source ledger;
10. rights ledger for quoted text and visual assets;
11. publication QA record.

## Visual inheritance

Religion Research inherits the approved Prince' Gutt world rather than creating a generic "occult" identity:

- deep black;
- wine/burgundy;
- aged gold;
- paper, metal, wood, studio/street texture;
- cinematic shadow, grain and real-light photography;
- sparse editorial composition;
- real/authorized Prince' Gutt assets first;
- green only as a rare signature accent.

Avoid neon occultism, AI-generated pseudo-manuscripts, plastic gold, excessive sacred geometry and generic mystical stock imagery.

Sacred symbols MUST be used with contextual accuracy rather than as decoration.

## Proposed series architecture

Working umbrella: **Cadernos do Príncipe — Tradição Universal**.

A volume should normally center one defensible thesis rather than summarize an entire religion.

Example volume families:

- Hermes — mente, correspondência, logos and cosmos;
- Pitágoras — número, harmonia, disciplina and cosmos;
- The Golden Rule — reciprocity across traditions;
- Logos — word, reason and creative ordering;
- Ascent — purification, knowledge and return;
- Number & Temple — measure, proportion and sacred architecture;
- Death & Rebirth — initiatory patterns without claiming identical doctrines;
- Silence — apophasis, contemplation and initiatory restraint.

## Acceptance criteria

The foundation is accepted when:

- the religion corpus is logically separated from artist/marketing evidence;
- claim typing prevents synthesis from masquerading as fact;
- copyright status is publication-gated;
- an e-book can be traced from source -> evidence -> claim -> comparative bridge -> manuscript section;
- visual output remains recognizably Prince' Gutt;
- the first seed video is represented as a source before interpretation;
- the first e-book can be produced without copying a protected modern work.
